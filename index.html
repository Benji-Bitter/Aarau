The story.

Why Aarau exists

A typeface built to be noticed slowly.

Aarau began with a frustration: too many modern sans serifs either disappear completely or try too hard to prove they are different. The goal was to sit in the space between those two extremes.

It needed to feel familiar enough to use every day, but considered enough that a brand, interface or publication could build a voice around it. Not loud. Not anonymous. Just deliberate.

01

Start with restraint.

The foundation is neo-grotesk: clear forms, practical proportions and a rhythm that does not fight the words. Aarau avoids decoration for its own sake. Its identity comes from the relationship between shapes rather than one attention-seeking letter.

02

Let details do the work.

The personality lives in small decisions: how a terminal finishes, how open a counter feels, how letters sit beside one another, and how those choices hold together across a whole family. Up close, those details matter. In a paragraph, they become rhythm.

03

Design one voice, not nine fonts.

Aarau runs from Thin 100 to Black 900, but the intention is continuity. Weight should change the volume of the typeface without changing its character. The variable axis lets that voice move continuously instead of forcing every use into a fixed preset.

04

Make it useful first.

The family is meant for real work: interfaces, identities, editorial layouts, packaging, presentations and everyday communication. Large or small, quiet or assertive, Aarau should keep the same underlying tone.
