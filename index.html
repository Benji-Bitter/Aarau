The story.

Quietly distinctive

Neutral at first glance.
Distinct in the details.

Aarau started with a simple idea: a typeface should be able to carry an identity without overpowering it. It should feel considered, useful and familiar, while still having enough character to be remembered.

Rather than chasing novelty, Aarau focuses on proportion, rhythm and restraint. The personality lives in the small decisions — how letters sit together, how weight changes across the family, and how the typeface holds its voice from interface text to oversized headlines.

The result is a neo-grotesk designed to work quietly in the background when it needs to, and step forward when the words need presence.

CalmMade to support the message instead of competing with it.
PreciseControlled spacing, proportion and weight give the family its rhythm.
FlexibleNine weights and a variable axis let one voice move across many contexts.
